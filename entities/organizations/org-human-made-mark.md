# The Human Made Mark

The Human Made Mark is a UK-based certification initiative for film, television, and commercial productions made exclusively by human artists. Incorporated as a private limited company (The Human Made Mark Ltd) in June 2025 and publicly launched on 23 April 2026 at the 29,000-year-old Pech Merle cave paintings in the Lot region of France, it issues a consumer-facing trust mark — analogous to the FairTrade label — that appears as an on-screen ident before a certified film and as a unique numbered certification in the end credits. The mark signals to audiences that every human artist involved in the production was protected from AI replacement.

## Founding

The organization was founded by British writer-director [William Grave](../persons/person-william-grave.md), whose films have appeared on BBC News and received international festival recognition, and [Eric Gruber](../persons/person-eric-gruber.md), a British-Canadian business director and former Toronto International Film Festival (TIFF) executive. Aanchal Kapoor, an entertainment lawyer, [built the contractual and legal framework](https://www.skwigly.co.uk/the-human-made-mark-launch/) behind the organization's verification platform.

The founders established the initiative in direct response to the accelerating displacement of human creative professionals by generative AI across film and television production pipelines — spanning visual effects, animation, music composition, and on-screen performance. Their core insight: the Fair Trade certification model, which had enabled ethically sourced goods to earn market premium by making their provenance legible at the point of purchase, could be applied to make the human-versus-AI distinction in filmmaking visible and actionable for audiences.

## Certification

To receive the mark, a producer or director submits a package of materials to The Human Made Mark's verification team: call sheets identifying cast and crew, behind-the-scenes photographs, a full credits list, a private viewing link, and a [signed legal declaration](https://www.animationforadults.com/2026/05/anti-ai-human-made-mark-verification.html) confirming that no human artist was replaced by AI. The organization acts as a third-party independent verifier, with reviews typically completed within one week.

Certified productions receive two trust marks: a distinctive on-screen ident before the film begins and a unique numbered certification in the end credits — a fingerprint-like number that makes each certification individually traceable. For commercials and branded content, the mark appears as a watermark on the finished piece. Certification at launch covered live-action and stop-motion animation, with planned expansion to additional animation techniques. The mark is registered as a UK trademark (No. UK00004237071).

## Industry supporters

Before the April 2026 public launch, The Human Made Mark attracted endorsements from senior Academy Award-winning and -nominated craft practitioners: production designer Tamara Deverell (Oscar winner), cinematographer [Dan Laustsen](https://www.skwigly.co.uk/the-human-made-mark-launch/) (three-time Oscar nominee), and costume designers Ruth E. Carter (Oscar winner, *Black Panther*) and Deborah L. Scott (Oscar winner, *Titanic*). Actor and voice talent Ruby Barker also joined as an early supporter. The design of the mark itself was created by London design agency Monday Nights (Matt Caldwell and Alice Fraser).

The organization ran an Oscar-season campaign — "Every Artist Makes Their Mark" — drawing further industry endorsements through social media. Co-founder Gruber described the mark as the "Michelin Star of human craft," positioning the certification as an aspirational quality signal rather than a minimum compliance threshold.
