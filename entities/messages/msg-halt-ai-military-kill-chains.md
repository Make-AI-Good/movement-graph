# Halt AI in Military Kill Chains

**"Halt AI in military kill chains"** is the central demand of the ["Joint Statement on AI in Warfare"](https://www.amnesty.org/en/documents/amr04/1146/2026/en/) — published by [Access Now](../organizations/org-access-now.md) under the headline ["AI-accelerated warfare must stop"](https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/ai-accelerated-warfare-must-stop/) — issued on 15 June 2026 by more than 200 civil society organisations and individuals, co-drafted by [Access Now](../organizations/org-access-now.md) and [Amnesty International](../organizations/org-amnesty-international.md). The statement calls on technology companies and states to ["halt the provision of AI systems for use in the military kill chain"](https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/joint-statement-on-ai-in-warfare/) and to ensure that other AI systems they provide do not cause or contribute to violations of international humanitarian law and international human rights law. Its signatories include the [Campaign to Stop Killer Robots](../organizations/org-stop-killer-robots.md), the [World Council of Churches](../organizations/org-world-council-of-churches.md), the [Association for Progressive Communications](../organizations/org-apc.md), Article 36, and more than 130 civil society organisations and 160 individuals, including current and former employees of Google, OpenAI, and Google DeepMind.

## The demand

The statement targets four categories of AI system for [immediate halting](https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/joint-statement-on-ai-in-warfare/): AI decision-support systems, target generation systems, remote biometric surveillance, and multimodal AI models including large language models. Anthropic's Claude, Palantir's Maven Smart System, Google, OpenAI, Microsoft, and Amazon are named. The statement warns that claimed "human-in-the-loop" safeguards risk becoming "a means of [rubber-stamping killing at greatly accelerated speed and scale](https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/joint-statement-on-ai-in-warfare/)" — eroding IHL's foundational principles of distinction, proportionality, and precaution. States are called to halt AI tools in military targeting operations, ensure IHL and IHRL compliance, and disclose current AI deployment in armed hostilities.

## Connection to the broader movement

The statement extends two converging threads in this corpus. First, the autonomous-weapons treaty advocacy the [Stop Killer Robots](msg-stop-killer-robots.md) coalition and the [meaningful human control](msg-meaningful-human-control.md) framing have carried through the CCW, GGE, and UNGA cycle since 2012 — the 2026 statement transposing that campaign's treaty-track demand into an AI-company-supply-chain accountability register, naming specific products and contractors rather than categories of weapons. Second, the tech-worker-conscience and civil-society supply-chain lineage the [Google-Maven framing](msg-google-not-in-the-business-of-war.md) established in 2018 — the 2026 statement extending that demand from internal-worker dissent to a multi-NGO coalition addressed to [the same named companies](https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/joint-statement-on-ai-in-warfare/) in a different war context eight years on.
