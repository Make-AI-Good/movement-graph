# "Stop the AI Race"

**Stop the AI Race** is the founding framing and protest banner of the [Stop the AI Race](../organizations/org-stop-the-ai-race.md) movement — a conditional-pause demand crystallised by [Michaël Trazzi](../persons/person-michael-trazzi.md) in September 2025 and carried into street protest through three major actions in San Francisco in 2026. The message's condensed form is simultaneously the campaign name, the march slogan, and the movement's strategic premise: the frontier AI labs are engaged in a competitive race each justifies on coordination grounds, and stopping it requires not unilateral restraint but a mutual conditional commitment.

## Origin and core demand

The framing cohered around the [September 2025 hunger strike](https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/68614/1/meet-protesters-hunger-strike-prevent-ai-catastrophe-google-deepmind-london) Trazzi conducted outside Google DeepMind's London office. Its demand, addressed directly to CEO Demis Hassabis and subsequently to Dario Amodei (Anthropic), Sam Altman (OpenAI), and Elon Musk (xAI): publicly commit that your lab will pause frontier model development [if every other major lab in the world credibly does the same](https://stoptherace.ai/). The conditional structure is the message's load-bearing innovation. Where the earlier [Pause Giant AI Experiments](msg-pause-giant-ai-experiments.md) framing asked for a six-month unilateral pause and the [AI moratorium](msg-ai-moratorium.md) framing demands an open-ended halt, this message accepts that competitive pressure is a real coordination constraint — and proposes to dissolve it through a verified mutual pledge rather than override it. The movement's own formulation: "We're not asking anyone to stop alone. Just to say: if the others pause, I will too."

The framing operationalises through a three-stage strategic framework: Stage 1, secure individual CEO commitments to a conditional pause; Stage 2, translate those into a US-China conditional-pause agreement; Stage 3, establish an international pause backed by verification mechanisms — AI chip tracking and compute (FLOP) caps — drawn from a MIRI Technical Governance Team proposal.

## Propagation

[PauseAI](../organizations/org-pauseai.md) explicitly endorsed the conditional-pause framing through its support of both 2026 marches, with [Holly Elmore](../persons/person-holly-elmore.md) keynoting the March 21 action alongside Dr. David Krueger (Evitable), Nate Soares (MIRI), and Will Fithian (UC Berkeley). [Stop AI](../organizations/org-stop-ai.md) participated in the same coalition marches, representing the moratorium-aligned flank of the broader AI-pause movement that joined the conditional framing's street mobilization while maintaining its own harder demand. The July 11 2026 thirteen-organization coalition — including the National Union of Healthcare Workers and Food and Water Watch alongside the core AI-safety groups — carried the slogan as the march's operational banner, the broadest coalition yet assembled under this framing.
