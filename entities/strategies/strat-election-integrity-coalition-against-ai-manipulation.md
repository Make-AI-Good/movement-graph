# Election-integrity coalition against AI manipulation

A civil-society coalition organises against the deployment of AI in elections — AI-generated deepfake political ads, voice-cloned candidate robocalls, automated voter-suppression texting, algorithmic ad-targeting of contested demographics with generative disinformation, chatbot impersonation of election-official communications, and generative content flooding of local political information ecosystems. The vehicle is a coalition running on the *election cycle*: an election-cycle war-room monitoring AI-generated content at scale, a rapid-response documentation and takedown pipeline coordinated with platforms and election officials, a legal-track pipeline running civil and criminal complaints against illegal AI-manipulated content, a media-cycle infrastructure that names and shames named deployers, and a poll-worker and election-official education programme so on-the-ground officials can recognise and respond to AI-manipulated incidents in real time. The reference models are the Election Protection coalition (Lawyers' Committee for Civil Rights Under Law, ACLU, NAACP LDF running the 866-OUR-VOTE infrastructure across US elections since 2001), the Voter Protection Program network of state coalitions, and the Global Network Initiative's election-integrity working groups.

An actor chooses this strategy because AI-in-elections is a distinct movement-side surface with distinct organising demands: **cycle-timed**, requiring war-room-cadence coordination the movement's usual campaign apparatus does not carry; **platform-facing**, requiring rapid-response takedown and provenance-verification coordination with platforms that policy-track lobbying does not produce on election-cycle timelines; **cross-partisan-fragile**, requiring a coalition register that can hold across the movement's coalitional fault-lines when AI-manipulated content targets candidates of different parties; and **election-official-facing**, requiring on-the-ground poll-worker and election-official education that framing and litigation strategies do not deliver into the field. The strategy also converts each election cycle into a permanent public record of AI-manipulation incidents, deployer identities, and response effectiveness the movement can carry forward into policy work the following year — the cycle produces the evidence base every subsequent regulatory demand rests on.

It trades continuity for cycle intensity. An election-cycle coalition mobilises hard during the cycle and drops off between cycles; the organising infrastructure atrophies between mobilisations, funders shift attention to non-election work, and the war-room capability has to be rebuilt each cycle from partially-assembled staffing. The strategy is also **structurally reactive**: it operates on content and behaviours the vendor or deployer has already deployed, and its lever against a large-scale AI-generated disinformation flood is limited to takedown-and-shame, which do not scale with generative volume. And AI-in-elections is a contested framing zone between the movement's coalitional partners: rapid-response coordination that names one party's operatives more than another's fractures the coalition mid-cycle, and rapid-response coordination that treats deployers symmetrically fails to name the asymmetric deployment patterns the movement's constituency knows are real.

## Ecology

**Complementary to [electoral endorsement and candidate questionnaire on AI](strat-electoral-endorsement-and-candidate-questionnaire-on-ai.md), distinct in register.** Electoral endorsement is *pro*-active: it rates candidates on their AI positions and mobilises single-issue-voter turnout on AI grounds. Election-integrity is *de*-fensive: it protects the election from AI-manipulation of the electorate regardless of any candidate's AI position. The two together are the movement's two-arm election-cycle stance — one arm shapes what candidates promise on AI, the other arm protects the process by which promises translate into votes.

**Feeds into [AI-specific criminal-code amendments](strat-ai-specific-criminal-code-amendments.md) and [coalition lobbying of binding regional regulation](strat-coalition-lobbying-of-binding-regional-regulation.md).** The election-cycle documentation the coalition produces is the evidentiary base every legislative campaign for deepfake-election-content laws, AI-generated-political-ad disclosure requirements, and voice-cloning-in-elections criminal statutes rests on. Legislative work between cycles is where the election-integrity coalition's documentation converts into binding rules; without a defensive-arm coalition producing the evidence, the legislative arm is running on anecdote.

**Register-adjacent to [counter-narrative framing](strat-counter-narrative-framing.md).** Both operate in the discourse register; both counter AI-produced content. The distinguishing question is *venue*: counter-narrative framing runs in the general media / cultural conversation on AI's meaning; election-integrity runs specifically inside election processes with election-cycle timelines, election-official interlocutors, and campaign-finance-law scaffolding. The disciplines and coalitions are distinct.
