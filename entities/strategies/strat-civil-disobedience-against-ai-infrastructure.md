# Civil disobedience against AI infrastructure

Escalated direct action targeting AI physical and organisational infrastructure — sit-ins at company headquarters, blockades of data-centre construction sites, occupations of tech-conference venues, obstruction of hardware-shipment routes, disruption of investor events — where arrest is expected and the willingness to accept legal consequences is the tactic's core credibility. Precedent lineages include ACT UP's FDA occupations and die-ins, Standing Rock's Dakota Access blockade, Extinction Rebellion's sustained road-blocking, the Movement for Black Lives' highway occupations, and the sustained lunch-counter and freedom-rides tradition of the civil-rights movement.

An actor chooses this strategy when procedural channels have visibly failed and the movement's credibility depends on demonstrating that a constituency will *physically* stand in the way of ongoing harm. Escalated CD converts diffuse public concern into a photographic and legal record that news outlets, elected officials, and adjacent institutions cannot ignore. It creates a moral hierarchy inside the movement that pulls timid coalitional partners toward stronger positions (the "flanking effect"), converts the criminal-justice system's response into further evidence of asymmetric power, and provides a decisive answer to the industry's claim that opposition is elite or armchair — the arrest photograph names the constituency's willingness to bear cost.

It trades broad palatability for symbolic force. CD alienates institutional and reputational partners with liability exposure, imposes real legal jeopardy on participants (which selects for the most-invested and can narrow the constituency), and can produce backlash framings the industry exploits ("radicals" versus "reasonable stakeholders"). Sustained CD requires legal-support infrastructure (bail funds, defence counsel, jail-support), non-violence discipline that scales poorly under stress, and a strategic understanding of *when* to escalate — a movement that goes to arrest too early has nowhere to escalate to; one that goes too late loses the moment.

## Ecology

**Escalation from [mass protest for AI moratorium](strat-mass-protest-for-ai-moratorium.md).** Mass permitted marches are the entry rung; CD is the next rung up. A movement running only permitted mobilisation has ceded the escalation ladder; a movement running only CD has no bench to escalate from.

**Concrete sites often are [local fights against AI data centres](strat-local-fight-against-ai-data-centers.md).** A blockade of an active construction site is one specific form of the tactic; the broader strategy also encompasses corporate-HQ, conference-venue, and hardware-supply-chain sites.

**Feeds [personalised executive accountability campaign](strat-personalized-executive-accountability-campaign.md).** CD at an executive's home, office, or public appearance is a distinct sub-tactic that binds the CD strategy to individual-accountability work.
