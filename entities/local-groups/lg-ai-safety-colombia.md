# AI Safety Colombia

AI Safety Colombia is a Bogotá-based community group building AI safety capacity in Colombia and across Latin America through researcher training, hackathon programmes, and public engagement with AI governance. Founded by Jose Gelves — a political scientist and AI governance researcher — the group's central activity is a [weekly Friday reading group conducted in Spanish](https://aisafetycolombia.org/en) and open to participants of all backgrounds, alongside research sprints run in partnership with Apart Research. The AISafety.com global communities directory lists AI Safety Colombia as "Active."

## Founding and structure

AI Safety Colombia was founded by **Jose Gelves**, who organizes it with a volunteer-heavy structure supplemented by grants and institutional partnerships. The core team includes **Alejandro Acelas** as a regular advisor and a mentor and speaker network drawn from Colombian institutions and international networks — including **Monica Ulloa** (Carreras con Impacto / IDB Lab), **Diego Gomez** (Google), and researchers at Colombian institutions such as ILAPS, Quantil, BIP, and CCIT. On-site logistics for major events are coordinated by **Deiver Romero** and **Santiago Ramírez**.

The group's key institutional partnerships include Apart Research (hackathon and sprint convener), [BlueDot Impact](../organizations/org-bluedot-impact.md) (course funding and curriculum infrastructure), Kairos/Pathfinder (mentorship and AI safety field-building), and Coefficient Giving (technical AI safety research funding). For its September 2026 sprint, AI Safety Colombia co-organized with **Young AI Leaders Bogotá**, the **Semillero de Modelos Generativos UNAL** (a student research group at Universidad Nacional de Colombia), and the **IEEE Computer Society Uniandes** chapter at Universidad de los Andes — reflecting growing university-rooted infrastructure in Bogotá.

## Programmes

AI Safety Colombia runs its community through three main formats.

**Friday reading group.** The group's ongoing backbone is a weekly reading group meeting every Friday, [conducted in Spanish and open to all levels of background](https://aisafetycolombia.org/en). The Spanish-language format directly addresses the language barrier separating Colombia's technical and policy talent from the predominantly English-language global AI safety literature.

**Research hackathons (Apart Research partnership).** AI Safety Colombia serves as the Bogotá hub for Apart Research's research sprint series. At the [Global South AI Safety Hackathon](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/global-south-ais-hackathon-2026-06-19-to-2026-06-21) (19–21 June 2026), the Bogotá hub attracted [33 participants](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/Kxd58zbWMJwtDmXQ9/running-the-bogota-hub-of-apart-s-global-south-ai-safety) and produced 25 research submissions — yielding 4 of the 13 global recognitions awarded (2 Latin America winners at USD 1,000 each; 2 honorable mentions). Colombian submissions represented 17 of 216 entries globally (7.9%), claiming all three Latin America winner slots. Speakers and judges came from OpenAI, UC Berkeley, UC Santa Cruz, Google, UNDP, and UNESCO alongside Colombian research institutions.

AI Safety Colombia subsequently co-organized the **AI Incident Response Sprint** ([September 11–13 2026](https://aisafetycolombia.org/en/hackathon/), Chicó, Bogotá) — a five-track weekend hackathon focused on building response protocols for frontier AI safety incidents, co-convened globally by Apart Research and [CeSIA](../organizations/org-cesia.md) (France's Centre pour la Sécurité de l'IA). The five tracks — containment standards, incident analysis, regulatory response, communication strategy, and an open track — distributed USD 2,000 in prizes across winning teams.

**Community engagement.** Beyond formal programmes, AI Safety Colombia maintains a WhatsApp community with ongoing entry-level opportunities, 1-on-1 mentoring, and regular discussion dinners and public talks on AI governance and safety in Bogotá. Post-hackathon activities included reunion events and LinkedIn community-building to sustain participant cohorts between sprint events.

## Place in the movement

AI Safety Colombia is the corpus's first entry for a Colombia-based AI safety community group, closing the LatAm geo-gap in the insider-community layer beyond Argentina. It joins [BAISH](lg-baish.md) (Buenos Aires) in the corpus's Latin American AI safety representation, and sits in the same insider-community pattern as [AI Safety 東京](lg-ai-safety-tokyo.md), [Singapore AI Safety Hub](lg-singapore-ai-safety-hub.md), [NTU AI Safety](lg-ai-safety-taiwan.md), [AI Safety South Africa](lg-ai-safety-south-africa.md), and [Berlin AI Safety](lg-ai-safety-berlin.md) — groups whose central activity is building the local practitioner pipeline through reading groups, workshops, and research mentoring rather than public-facing campaign or protest work.

Its distinguishing feature within this cohort is its explicitly civic and governance orientation at the founding level. Gelves's background in political science and AI governance, the group's engagement with policymakers and democratic-process dimensions of AI governance, and its involvement with Colombian institutional actors (CCIT, ILAPS, government contacts) give AI Safety Colombia a policy-aware civic capacity-building shape that complements BAISH's technically-anchored research hub model across the same regional network.

Both groups serve as hub sites in the Apart Research sprint infrastructure alongside EA Brazil (São Paulo), AISMX (Mexico), and the Santa Cruz Hub (Bolivia) — a Latin American AI safety infrastructure that is increasingly coordinated at the regional level.
