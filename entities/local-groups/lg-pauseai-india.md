# PauseAI India

PauseAI India is the Indian national chapter of [PauseAI](../organizations/org-pauseai.md), the international AI-safety movement calling for a coordinated, treaty-backed pause on the training of the most powerful general-purpose AI systems. The chapter is the corpus's first South Asian entry in the PauseAI federation cluster — extending the federation's documented reach into South Asia alongside its in-corpus chapters across Africa ([PauseAI Kenya](lg-pauseai-kenya.md), [PauseAI Nigeria](lg-pauseai-nigeria.md)), Europe ([PauseAI London](lg-pauseai-london.md), [PauseAI Germany](lg-pauseai-germany.md), and others), and the Americas ([PauseAI Bay Area](lg-pauseai-bay-area.md), [PauseAI NYC](lg-pauseai-nyc.md), [PauseAI Canada](lg-pauseai-canada.md)).

## Formation and structure

[PauseAI Global's 12 November 2025 newsletter](https://pauseai.substack.com/p/we-screened-an-ai-safety-documentary) — under a "New National Chapters" section — reported that "Volunteers in Canada, Serbia, Romania, and India have recently formed their own groups, taking us to a total of 15 worldwide." This is the primary documented source for the chapter's formation, placing it in late 2025 alongside three other newly-formed national chapters. No chapter lead has been named on PauseAI Global's public-facing channels; no dedicated chapter website or social-media account has been documented.

At the time of this entry's initial draft (September 2026), India is absent from [PauseAI Global's communities directory](https://pauseai.info/communities), which lists chapters across nineteen countries. The chapter's unlisted status in the public directory is consistent with its early formation stage — analogous to [PauseAI Nigeria](lg-pauseai-nigeria.md)'s initial WhatsApp-only listing before it launched a dedicated website at [pauseai.ng](https://pauseai.ng/).

## India AI Impact Summit context

[PauseAI Global ran a campaign around the India AI Impact Summit](https://pauseai.info/india-summit-2026) — the third major international AI summit, held at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi on 19–20 February 2026 — calling on policymakers and summit delegates to prioritise AI safety commitments, noting that "In India, there is a real risk that safety disappears from the agenda entirely." The campaign page does not name a PauseAI India chapter organiser or any India-specific chapter action. The February 2025 Paris AI Action Summit protest wave — PauseAI's most internationally documented coordinated action to date, spanning eighteen cities — lists no Indian city among its participants.

## Place in the movement

PauseAI India is the corpus's first PauseAI chapter in South Asia and the corpus's first AI-safety or AI-pause movement entry at the local-group level for India, a country whose government has publicly targeted top-three global AI superpower status by 2047 and launched a national AI infrastructure programme. The chapter's formation registers a grassroots AI-safety presence in India at a time when the country's official posture is pro-development rather than pro-pause — a positioning the corpus does not otherwise have a South Asian anchor for. Among the corpus's South Asia entries, this chapter opens the AI-pause arc alongside existing labour and digital-rights entries ([org-ifat-india](../organizations/org-ifat-india.md) and [org-digital-rights-foundation](../organizations/org-digital-rights-foundation.md)). The India AI Impact Summit's New Delhi hosting in February 2026 gives the chapter a potentially significant domestic context for its first phases of public mobilisation; whether organised chapter participation materialised at the summit is not documented at draft time.
