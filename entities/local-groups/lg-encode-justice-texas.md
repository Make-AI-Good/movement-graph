# Encode Justice Texas

Encode Justice Texas (EJ-TX) is the Texas state chapter of [Encode Justice](../organizations/org-encode-justice.md), the youth-led international AI-policy advocacy organization founded by Sneha Revanur in 2020. With more than 30 members drawn from high school and college student networks across Texas, the chapter operates statewide and is organized around three internal teams: a Technical Research team, an Education team, and a Policy team.

## Place in the Encode Justice network

EJ-TX is one of more than forty U.S. state chapters in Encode Justice's distributed student-organizing network. Unlike the [North Carolina](lg-encode-justice-north-carolina.md), [Oregon](lg-encode-justice-oregon.md), and [Georgia](lg-encode-justice-georgia.md) chapters, the Texas chapter has not been documented as an Electronic Frontier Alliance member — the EFA itself [concluded on 20 November 2025](https://efa.eff.org/) — but the chapter is a contributing participant in the [StateAI Project](https://encodeai.org/the-stateai-project-brought-to-you-by-our-u-s-chapters/), Encode's national chapter-driven initiative to map the AI and technology legislative landscape across U.S. states. The project's outputs include state-specific policy briefs, key-stakeholder profiles, and recommendations for lawmakers and advocates; its method is the local chapter supplying the on-the-ground read of each state's legislative terrain. Siri Ganapathineedi, the chapter's Director of Policy, is the named Texas contributor.

## Chapter structure and activities

The chapter's three teams mirror the standard Encode model applied to Texas contexts:

- **Technical Research team:** researches the societal impacts of AI, focusing on ethics, bias mitigation, and equity; collaborates on white papers and policy recommendations for ethical AI practices.
- **Education team:** develops AI ethics curriculum for workshops at hackathons, schools, and other forums; runs educational programs on ethical AI for student and youth audiences, including workshops, webinars, and curricula.
- **Policy team:** monitors and engages with AI-related legislative and regulatory developments in Texas, supporting the chapter's lobbying and advocacy work at the state level.

Public contact runs through the chapter's [Instagram account](https://www.instagram.com/encodejusticetexas/) (@encodejusticetexas) and a [chapter website](https://ejtexas.weblium.site/) at ejtexas.weblium.site.

## Texas AI policy context

The chapter operates in a Texas AI policy environment that has changed significantly since 2024. The Texas Responsible Artificial Intelligence Governance Act (TRAIGA), signed by Governor Greg Abbott on 22 June 2025 and effective 1 January 2026, made [Texas the second U.S. state to enact comprehensive AI regulation](https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/insights/blogs/wilmerhale-privacy-and-cybersecurity-law/20250721-texas-enacts-new-ai-law) after Colorado. TRAIGA established intent-based liability for AI misuse — covering discrimination, deepfakes, child exploitation, and constitutional-rights violations — created the Texas Artificial Intelligence Council, and stood up a regulatory sandbox program. For a chapter committed to state-level AI policy advocacy, the 2025 Texas legislative session represented a major inflection point in the chapter's operating environment and a concrete site for the StateAI Project's analytical work.

## Posture in the movement

Encode Justice Texas is a clear in-scope local group: a youth-led, Texas-wide chapter of an in-corpus national organization that engages non-AI publics — Texas high school and college students, youth civic audiences, and state-level policymakers — in shaping how AI is developed and deployed. Its participation in the StateAI Project gives the chapter a documented output channel linking on-the-ground Texas engagement to the national organization's legislative-intelligence work. EJ-TX is the first Encode Justice chapter entry in the corpus with a south-central U.S. geographic anchor, complementing the existing Encode chapter coverage in the mid-Atlantic ([New York](lg-encode-justice-new-york.md)), Southeast ([North Carolina](lg-encode-justice-north-carolina.md), [Georgia](lg-encode-justice-georgia.md)), and West Coast ([California](lg-encode-justice-california.md), [Oregon](lg-encode-justice-oregon.md)).
