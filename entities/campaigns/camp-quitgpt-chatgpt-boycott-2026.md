# QuitGPT — ChatGPT Consumer Boycott Campaign (2026–ongoing)

QuitGPT is a grassroots consumer boycott campaign [launched in late January 2026](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/02/10/1132577/a-quitgpt-campaign-is-urging-people-to-cancel-chatgpt-subscriptions/) at [quitgpt.org](https://quitgpt.org) by a loose coalition of democracy activists — dozens of left-leaning teens and twentysomethings across the US, including pro-democracy organizers, climate activists, and cyber libertarians, many remaining anonymous due to concerns about OpenAI's use of subpoenas against nonprofits. The campaign urges users to cancel ChatGPT Plus subscriptions ($20/month), delete the app, and share the boycott publicly. Its central message: ["ChatGPT is Trump's biggest donor, and ICE uses ChatGPT. It's time to Quit."](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/quitgpt-campaign-wants-ditch-chatgpt-192502646.html)

## Origins and triggers

Two triggers shaped the late-January 2026 launch. First, [public records showed](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/quitgpt-campaign-wants-ditch-chatgpt-192502646.html) OpenAI president Greg Brockman and his wife had donated a combined $25 million to the MAGA Inc. pro-Trump super PAC, and CEO Sam Altman had donated $1 million to Trump's inaugural fund. Second, activists surfaced ICE's use of a ChatGPT-4-powered tool for résumé screening — a flashpoint following ICE agents fatally shooting two people in Minneapolis in January 2026. The founding coalition describes its motivation as needing to [make "a big enough stink for OpenAI" that all AI companies consider whether they can enable "Trump and ICE and authoritarianism"](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/02/10/1132577/a-quitgpt-campaign-is-urging-people-to-cancel-chatgpt-subscriptions/) without consequence.

## Pentagon deal escalation (February 2026)

The campaign's critical surge came on [February 28, 2026](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/03/02/cancel-chatgpt-ai-boycott-surges-after-openai-pentagon-military-deal), when Sam Altman announced OpenAI had signed a Pentagon contract within hours of Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei publicly refusing the same deal. Amodei had stated that "in a narrow set of cases, we believe AI can undermine, rather than defend, democratic values," and the Pentagon declined to limit use to non-surveillance and non-weapons applications. OpenAI's contract permitted its models to be used for "any lawful purpose," including domestic mass surveillance and autonomous weapons. [ChatGPT daily uninstalls spiked 295% on February 28](https://www.fox5dc.com/news/boycott-movement-against-chatgpt-grows-amid-openais-pentagon-deal) relative to the baseline rate. By March 3, organizers claimed 1.5 to 2.5 million participants through subscription cancellations, app deletions, or pledge sign-ups.

## In-person actions

**San Francisco headquarters protest — March 3, 2026.** QuitGPT organized a rally outside OpenAI's San Francisco headquarters, [drawing approximately 50–75 people](https://www.fox5dc.com/news/boycott-movement-against-chatgpt-grows-amid-openais-pentagon-deal) who chanted through megaphones and held signs reading "Sam Altman is watching you" and "QuitGPT." Actor Mark Ruffalo publicly endorsed the campaign on Instagram around this period, [garnering over 1.6 million likes](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/quitgpt-campaign-wants-ditch-chatgpt-192502646.html). OpenAI's CEO stated the company had built "technical safeguards" into the Pentagon contract to prevent misuse, a claim described as meeting widespread skepticism among campaign participants.

**DC lobbying office occupation — August 10, 2026.** More than 30 student activists organized jointly by QuitGPT, the [Sunrise Movement](https://www.sunrisemovement.org/), and the Young Democratic Socialists of America [occupied OpenAI's Washington DC lobbying office ("The Workshop")](https://www.commondreams.org/news/ai-protest), just blocks from the White House, for two hours starting at 12:30 PM ET. Activists unfurled banners reading "Stop Stealing Our Future" and held signs saying "OpenAI Bought My Senator." Police arrested 13 people for unlawful entry at 2:30 PM ET; all were released within two days. Sunrise Executive Director Aru Shiney-Ajay stated: "Tech billionaires are trying to rob us of our future." The action was part of a [coordinated "Dump Big Tech" wave across more than 20 states](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/protesters-arrested-protest-openai-washington-dc) during Congressional August recess, opposing OpenAI's lobbying campaign to preempt state AI regulation, secure legal immunity for AI-caused harms, and maintain Pentagon surveillance contracts. OpenAI's super PAC had injected over $140 million into the 2026 midterm elections.
