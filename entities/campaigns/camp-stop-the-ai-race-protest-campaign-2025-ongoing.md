# Stop the AI Race protest campaign (2025–ongoing)

[Stop the AI Race](../organizations/org-stop-the-ai-race.md), founded by filmmaker and former AI safety researcher [Michaël Trazzi](../persons/person-michael-trazzi.md), has run a direct-action campaign since September 2025 organized around a single conditional-pause demand: every major AI lab CEO must publicly commit to pausing frontier model development if every other major lab credibly does the same. The demand targets the competitive-pressure framing — that individual labs cannot slow unilaterally — by seeking a verifiable mutual pledge rather than unilateral restraint.

## Founding action: London hunger strike (September 2025)

The campaign opened with [Trazzi staging a hunger strike outside Google DeepMind's London office](https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/68614/1/meet-protesters-hunger-strike-prevent-ai-catastrophe-google-deepmind-london), addressing the conditional-pause demand directly to CEO Demis Hassabis. The action lasted approximately one week before Trazzi withdrew on medical advice after fainting; Denys Sheremet of Together Against AI joined and continued at the same site. A parallel hunger strike was staged by Guido Reichstadter outside Anthropic's San Francisco headquarters.

## US protest marches (2026)

On [21 March 2026](../events/event-stop-the-ai-race-sf-march-2026-03-21.md), Stop the AI Race organized its first US street march — from Anthropic's offices to OpenAI's to xAI's in San Francisco — drawing approximately 200 participants alongside [PauseAI](../organizations/org-pauseai.md) and [Stop AI](../organizations/org-stop-ai.md) in a coalition including MIRI, QuitGPT, and Evitable. The march was reported by the New York Times, the Washington Post, and The Atlantic.

On [11 July 2026](../events/event-stop-the-ai-race-sf-march-2026-07-11.md), a second march through San Francisco's Mission Bay and SoMa districts — past OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind — drew approximately 400 participants across a 13-organization coalition, [described as the first major AI protest in US history](https://www.dailycal.org/news/largest-ai-protest-in-american-history-sees-hundreds-march-on-downtown-san-francisco/article_8253b059-e793-4de4-a012-cc20ddc5ad69.html). The coalition broadened beyond the AI safety research community to include the National Union of Healthcare Workers and Food and Water Watch.

## Sustained occupation (July 2026–ongoing)

On [22 July 2026](../events/event-stop-the-ai-race-openai-occupation-2026-07-22.md), Stop the AI Race began a continuous daily occupation outside OpenAI's San Francisco headquarters; the action expanded to OpenAI's New York City office on 18 August 2026. Organizers cited over 1,000 employees from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind — including OpenAI's Chief Scientist — who publicly urged government support for international mechanisms to slow frontier AI development as evidence that pressure on lab leadership was producing internal movement.
