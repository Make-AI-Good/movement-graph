# SAG-AFTRA contract ratification with AI provisions (November 2023)

On [8 November 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_SAG-AFTRA_strike), [SAG-AFTRA](../organizations/org-sag-aftra.md) and the Alliance of Motion Picture and Television Producers (AMPTP) reached a tentative agreement, ending the actors' 118-day strike — the longest in the union's history against Hollywood studios. The deal secured the union's [first-ever contractual protections around AI technology](https://www.sagaftra.org/sag-aftra-members-approve-2023-tvtheatrical-contracts-tentative-agreement) for performers. The [SAG-AFTRA National Board approved the agreement on 10 November 2023 by 86%](https://www.sagaftra.org/sag-aftra-national-board-approves-tentative-agreement-recommends-ratification-2023-tvtheatrical) and recommended a yes vote; members [ratified the contract on 5 December 2023 by 78.33% on a 38.15% turnout](https://www.sagaftra.org/sag-aftra-members-approve-2023-tvtheatrical-contracts-tentative-agreement).

The AI architecture of the contract — detailed by [Perkins Coie](https://perkinscoie.com/insights/blog/generative-ai-movies-and-tv-how-2023-sag-aftra-and-wga-contracts-address-generative) — divides the AI-generated-content space into five categories: **employment-based digital replicas** (replicas of a performer created during employment); **independently created digital replicas** (built from existing photos or footage without active participation); **background-actor digital replicas**; **synthetic performers** (digitally created characters not resembling any real performer); and **digital alterations** (edits to recorded footage). Each category requires clear and conspicuous informed consent from the performer, with consent and compensation structured to the type of use. The contract committed the parties to ongoing dialogue on synthetic-performer compensation — explicitly deferring that question for future bargaining.

As the [Center for Democracy and Technology](https://cdt.org/insights/the-sag-aftra-strike-is-over-but-the-ai-fight-in-hollywood-is-just-beginning/) observed, the ratification closed round one of AI bargaining in Hollywood while opening the harder questions — training-data rights, synthetic-performer compensation, interaction with residuals — to the next contract cycle. Together with the [WGA](../organizations/org-wga.md)'s ratification in October 2023, the SAG-AFTRA deal completed the 2023 Hollywood labor arc as a field-defining template for creative-industry AI bargaining.
