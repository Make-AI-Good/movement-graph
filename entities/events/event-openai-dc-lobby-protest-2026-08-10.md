# OpenAI DC lobbying office occupation (10 August 2026)

On 10 August 2026, more than 30 student activists co-organized by QuitGPT, the Sunrise Movement, and the Young Democratic Socialists of America (YDSA) [occupied OpenAI's Washington DC lobbying office — "The Workshop," a few blocks from the White House](https://www.commondreams.org/news/ai-protest) — for two hours starting at 12:30 PM ET. Activists unfurled a banner reading "Stop Stealing Our Future" and held signs reading "OpenAI Bought My Senator." Police arrested 13 people at 2:30 PM ET for unlawful entry; all were released within two days.

## Action and demands

The occupation targeted OpenAI's DC lobbying operation at a moment when [OpenAI's super PAC had spent over $140 million on the 2026 midterm elections](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/protesters-arrested-protest-openai-washington-dc), and the company was lobbying to preempt state AI regulation, secure legal immunity for AI-caused harms, and maintain its Pentagon surveillance contracts. Sunrise Movement Executive Director Aru Shiney-Ajay [stated](https://www.commondreams.org/news/ai-protest): "Tech billionaires are trying to rob us of our future." Student organizer Raya Gupta said her peers [chose to risk arrest to bring the fight to OpenAI](https://www.commondreams.org/news/ai-protest), citing the absence of job security, clean water guarantees, and political power for their generation as long as elections "keep getting bought by Big Tech."

## Broader context — the "Dump Big Tech" wave

The DC occupation was the highest-profile action in a [coordinated "Dump Big Tech" wave of 20+ state actions](https://www.techbrew.com/stories/dump-big-tech-protest-palantir-google) organized during Congressional August recess. Targets across the wave included OpenAI, Palantir, and Google offices; elected officials in New York, New Jersey, California, Nevada, and Pennsylvania; and data center construction sites in Oregon and West Virginia. The shared demands: stricter AI regulation, an end to federal Big Tech partnerships, and energy and water usage caps for data centers. The DC action represented the escalation of the [QuitGPT — ChatGPT Consumer Boycott Campaign](../campaigns/camp-quitgpt-chatgpt-boycott-2026.md) from online consumer pressure into coordinated physical occupation and civil disobedience.
