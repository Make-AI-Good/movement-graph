# Fairly Trained certification launch (January 2024)

On [17 January 2024](https://venturebeat.com/ai/more-ethical-ai-fairly-trained-launches-to-certify-gen-ai-tools-trained-on-licensed-data), [Fairly Trained](../organizations/org-fairly-trained.md) launched the Licensed Model (L) certification — a nonprofit credentialing mark for generative AI developers that train exclusively on licensed data, without invoking fair-use exceptions or non-consensual data collection. The launch was the first movement-run, consumer-facing AI certification mark in the creator-consent space.

[Nine generative AI companies](https://www.fairlytrained.org/blog/fairly-trained-launches-certification-for-generative-ai-models-that-respect-creators-rights) received certification at launch — Beatoven.AI, Boomy, BRIA AI, Endel, LifeScore, Rightsify, Somms.ai, Soundful, and Tuney — all operating in audio, music, and image modalities. Founding industry supporters included Universal Music Group, the Association of American Publishers, the Association of Independent Music Publishers, Concord, and Pro Sound Effects. The certification applied a sliding-scale fee: $150 one-time plus $500 annually for smaller companies, up to $500 one-time plus $6,000 annually for companies exceeding $10M in revenue.

The binary certification standard — a model either uses only licensed data or it does not — was designed to make the consent divide legible to consumers and institutional partners, applying the Fair Trade insight to AI training-data practices.
