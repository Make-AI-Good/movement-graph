# Stop the AI Race OpenAI occupation (July 2026)

On 22 July 2026, [Stop the AI Race](../organizations/org-stop-the-ai-race.md) began a continuous daily occupation outside OpenAI's San Francisco headquarters at [550 Terry A Francois Blvd](https://stoptherace.ai/occupy/) — a peaceful sustained presence of concerned citizens, families, and researchers, launched eleven days after the [Stop the AI Race march on 11 July 2026](../events/event-stop-the-ai-race-sf-march-2026-07-11.md) drew approximately 400 people. The single demand: that Sam Altman publicly commit to pursuing an international AI treaty to coordinate a halt to frontier model development.

## Expansion to New York City

On [18 August 2026](https://stoptherace.ai/occupy/), the occupation extended to OpenAI's New York office at 295 Lafayette St, establishing a daily presence on both coasts. The San Francisco site runs 9am–6pm weekdays; the New York site runs 8:30am–6:30pm weekdays.

## Context

The occupation represents an escalation of Stop the AI Race's direct-action approach from periodic street marches to sustained daily presence at OpenAI's offices. During the occupation's first months, [over 1,000 employees from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind](https://stoptherace.ai/occupy/) — including OpenAI's Chief Scientist — urged US government support for international mechanisms to slow frontier AI development, an outcome organizers cited as evidence that pressure on lab leadership was producing internal movement.
