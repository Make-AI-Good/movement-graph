# PauseAI demonstration at the European Parliament (23 February 2026)

On 23 February 2026, [PauseAI](../organizations/org-pauseai.md) staged a demonstration at [Place du Luxembourg](https://pauseai.info/brussels-ep-protest-2026) in Brussels — the square directly outside the European Parliament — drawing approximately 100 demonstrators. The protest was the culminating public action of PauseCon Brussels (21–23 February 2026), a three-day organizer training and mobilization event for PauseAI chapter volunteers from across Europe.

## PauseCon Brussels

PauseCon Brussels ran 21–23 February at First Euroflat (Bd Charlemagne 50, Brussels), organised by [Joseph Miller](../persons/person-joseph-miller.md) and EllaH through PauseAI Events Global. The 87-person conference programme covered community-building strategy, social-media organizing, and flyering, and culminated on the final day in two public-facing actions: a conference inside the European Parliament in the morning and the Place du Luxembourg demonstration in the afternoon (15:30–16:30 CET).

## Parliamentary conference

The morning conference inside the European Parliament featured MEPs Ondřej Kolář, Saskia Bricmont, and Brando Benifei (primary architect of the EU AI Act), alongside European Parliament Vice-President Victor Negrescu, Professor Stuart Russell (University of California Berkeley; lead author of *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*), and Risto Uuk (Head of European Policy, Future of Life Institute). [Maxime Fournes](../persons/person-maxime-fournes.md) called for a pause in the development of superintelligence; Russell characterised the development trajectory as one towards loss of human control. Each of the MEPs present acknowledged catastrophic AI risks.

## Demands

PauseAI called on the EU to: (1) initiate negotiations for a global treaty to pause the development of frontier AI systems beyond the current capability frontier, conditional on demonstrable progress in AI safety and alignment research; (2) establish international oversight mechanisms including compute monitoring and safety evaluations; (3) champion democratic governance of AI.

## Outcomes

PauseCon participants subsequently met with their own MEPs to discuss the issues raised at the conference, and many volunteer projects were initiated over the weekend. Five days later, on [28 February 2026](../events/event-march-against-machines-london-2026-02-28.md), PauseAI UK co-organised the March Against the Machines in London alongside Pull the Plug — drawing 300–500 participants and characterised as the largest AI safety protest on record at that time. The two events together mark the [campaign's](../campaigns/camp-pauseai-international-protests-2023-ongoing.md) most concentrated European week of action to date.
