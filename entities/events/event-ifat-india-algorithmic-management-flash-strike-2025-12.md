# IFAT India algorithmic-management flash strike (25 December 2025)

On 25 December 2025, the [Indian Federation of App-based Transport Workers](../organizations/org-ifat-india.md) organized a [flash strike](https://progressive.international/wire/2025-12-29-indias-gig-workers-strike-for-dignity-and-protection/en/) in which approximately 40,000 delivery workers across Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, and Bengaluru refused orders on Swiggy, Zomato, Zepto, Blinkit, Amazon, and Flipkart. Over 2,000 workers joined a bike rally in Hyderabad. The action is an event in the [IFAT India gig-worker algorithmic-accountability campaign](../campaigns/camp-ifat-india-gig-worker-algorithmic-accountability-2021-ongoing.md), IFAT's sustained push combining direct industrial mobilization with public interest litigation before India's Supreme Court.

## Demands

Workers demanded fair wages, elimination of unsafe ten-minute delivery targets, an end to arbitrary algorithmic penalties, improved safety equipment, and comprehensive social security coverage. IFAT co-founder Shaik Salauddin framed the action as a response to workers being [pushed to the breaking point by unsafe work models, falling incomes, and the total absence of social protection](https://progressive.international/wire/2025-12-29-indias-gig-workers-strike-for-dignity-and-protection/en/) — connecting the specific algorithmic-penalty grievance to the broader statutory recognition gap the federation pursues through the Supreme Court petition.

## Outcome

No platform-side concessions were announced following the action.
