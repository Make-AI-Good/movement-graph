# Stop the AI Race march (San Francisco, 21 March 2026)

On 21 March 2026, [Stop the AI Race](../organizations/org-stop-the-ai-race.md) organized its first major US protest action: a street march in San Francisco tracing a route from Anthropic's offices to OpenAI's to xAI's, [drawing approximately 200 participants](https://decrypt.co/362129/protesters-rally-openai-anthropic-xai-offices). The coalition included the Machine Intelligence Research Institute, [PauseAI](../organizations/org-pauseai.md), [Stop AI](../organizations/org-stop-ai.md), QuitGPT, and Evitable.

## Demand and targets

The march carried the single conditional-pause demand central to Stop the AI Race: every major AI lab CEO must publicly commit to pausing frontier model development if every other major lab credibly does the same. The action publicly named Dario Amodei (Anthropic), Sam Altman (OpenAI), and Elon Musk (xAI) as the target recipients of the conditional commitment request.

## Speakers

[Speakers included](https://pauseai.info/stop-the-ai-race-march) [Holly Elmore](../persons/person-holly-elmore.md) (then Executive Director of PauseAI US), Dr. David Krueger (AI professor at the University of Montreal and founder of Evitable), Nate Soares (President of the Machine Intelligence Research Institute), and Will Fithian (UC Berkeley professor).

## Coverage and context

The march was [reported by the New York Times, the Washington Post, and The Atlantic](https://stoptherace.ai/), bringing the conditional-pause demand to national audiences for the first time. The action preceded the larger [Stop the AI Race march on 11 July 2026](../events/event-stop-the-ai-race-sf-march-2026-07-11.md), which drew approximately 400 participants, and came three weeks after the comparable [March Against the Machines](../events/event-march-against-machines-london-2026-02-28.md) in London.
