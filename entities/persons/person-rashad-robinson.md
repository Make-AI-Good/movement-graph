# Rashad Robinson

Civil rights organizer and longtime president of [Color of Change](../organizations/org-color-of-change.md), which he [led from May 2011](https://colorofchange.org/blog/internal_press/colorofchangeorg-announces-rashad-robinson-as-new/) through 2024. At Color of Change, Robinson directed the organization's tech-accountability campaigns: co-leading Stop Hate for Profit (2020), driving the Facebook Civil Rights Audit, and [testifying before the House Energy and Commerce subcommittee](https://colorofchange.org/press_release/rashad-robinson-testimony-at-house-energy-and-commerce-committee-hearing-on-holding-big-tech-accountable/) on algorithmic discrimination in December 2021. He was a direct interlocutor in negotiations with Facebook, Google, Twitter, and Airbnb leadership on racial bias in their platforms. Earned his BA in Political Science from Marymount University (2001); Atlantic Fellow for Racial Equity (2019) and adjunct lecturer at Georgetown University's McCourt School of Public Policy.

Before Color of Change, Robinson served as Communications Director for the Right to Vote Campaign, National Field Director at [FairVote (Center for Voting and Democracy)](https://docs.house.gov/meetings/IF/IF16/20211201/114268/HHRG-117-IF16-Bio-RobinsonR-20211201.pdf), and Senior Director of Media Programs at [GLAAD](https://www.glaad.org/releases/glaad-appoints-rashad-robinson-senior-director-media-programs) (2005–2011), where he initiated the Network Responsibility Index holding entertainment networks accountable for LGBTQ+ representation. He [resigned from Color of Change in October 2024](https://prismreports.org/2024/10/16/color-of-change-president-resigns-following-nlrb-ruling-on-layoffs/) following an NLRB ruling related to staff layoffs, and subsequently founded Rashad Robinson Advisors. Serves on the board of the Marguerite Casey Foundation.
