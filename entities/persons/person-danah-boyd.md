# danah boyd

Founder of [Data & Society Research Institute](../organizations/org-data-and-society.md) and Geri Gay Professor of Communication at Cornell University. boyd [founded Data & Society in 2014](https://datasociety.net/network-members/danah-boyd/) with seed funding from Microsoft Research, where she has held a concurrent role as partner researcher since 2009, serving as president of the institute through approximately 2022 before it transitioned to Executive Director Janet Haven. She holds a [BA in Computer Science from Brown University (2000), an MS from MIT Media Lab (2002), and a PhD in Information from UC Berkeley (2008)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danah_boyd), with her dissertation examining American teen sociality in networked publics.

Additional roles include founding director of Crisis Text Line, [AAAS Fellow, and trustee of the Computer History Museum](https://cals.cornell.edu/people/danah-boyd); she has previously held positions at Harvard's Berkman Center for Internet & Society, Georgetown University, NYU, Intel Research, Google, and Yahoo! Research. Her public-voice output — including foundational books on social media and data democracy, sustained public commentary through the apophenia blog, and her legacy as institutional architect of Data & Society — is tracked in the linked Voice entry.
