# Yeshimabeit Milner

Co-founder and CEO of [Data for Black Lives](../organizations/org-data-for-black-lives.md), which she [co-founded with Lucas Mason-Brown in November 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeshimabeit_Milner) after earning a BA in Africana Studies from Brown University. Milner began organizing against the school-to-prison pipeline as a high school senior at the Power U Center for Social Change in Miami. She [coined the term "Data Weapon"](https://d4bl.org/campaigns/2-no-more-data-weapons) — naming surveillance technologies such as gang databases, predictive risk scores, and facial recognition deployed against Black and Brown communities — and anchored D4BL's public campaigns in this frame, including the #NoMoreDataWeapons campaign (2021) and a [White House roundtable on abolishing FICO credit scores](https://d4bl.org/dispatch) in August 2023.

Holds fellowships from [Echoing Green](https://fellows.echoinggreen.org/fellow/yeshimabeit-milner/) and the Roddenberry Foundation (inaugural fellow, 2018, alongside founders of Black Lives Matter and Occupy Wall Street), and is an Ashoka Fellow. Received the [2021 John F. Kennedy New Frontier Award](https://d4bl.org/people/9-yeshimabeit-milner), the 2020 New York Times Good Tech Award (for D4BL's COVID-19 racial-impact database), and Forbes 30 Under 30 (2020). Serves on the board of the Highlander Research and Education Center.
