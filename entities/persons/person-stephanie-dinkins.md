# Stephanie Dinkins

Transmedia artist, educator, and Yayoi Kusama Professor of Art at Stony Brook University, where she founded the [Future Histories Studio](https://www.futurehistories.studio/about-us/our-people) — a community-centered lab at the intersections of art, technology, race, storytelling, and social justice. [MFA from the Maryland Institute College of Art; graduate of the Whitney Independent Study Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephanie_Dinkins).

Practice focuses on community-participatory platforms for dialogue about AI as it intersects race, gender, aging, and data sovereignty — specifically working with communities of color to co-create equitable AI ecosystems.

[Inaugural recipient of the LG Guggenheim Award (2023)](https://www.guggenheim.org/press-release/stephanie-dinkins-named-inaugural-recipient-of-the-lg-guggenheim-award) — first artist recognized under the LG Guggenheim Art and Technology Initiative, unrestricted honorarium of $100,000. [Named one of Time's 100 Most Influential People in AI (2023)](https://news.stonybrook.edu/university/professor-stephanie-dinkins-named-a-time-100-ai-innovator/). Also: [Creative Capital grantee (2019), Soros Equality Fellow (2018/19), Data and Society Research Institute Fellow, 2021 United States Artist Fellow, Knight Arts & Tech Fellow](https://aiartists.org/stephanie-dinkins); [Artist in Residence at Stanford HAI](https://hai.stanford.edu/people/stephanie-dinkins).

*Not the Only One* — ongoing multigenerational AI chatbot trained on oral histories from three generations of Dinkins's Black American family, designed to give AI cultural roots, deep history, and a Black-community perspective absent from mainstream AI training data.
