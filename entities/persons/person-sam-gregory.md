# Sam Gregory

Executive Director of [WITNESS](../organizations/org-witness.md) through August 2026, leading the organization for over 25 years at the intersection of video, technology, and human rights. [Co-created ProofMode](https://proofmode.org/blog/witness-techadvo) with the Guardian Project — the mobile cryptographic-authentication tool adding verifiable chain-of-custody metadata to photos and video for human rights defenders — and served as civil-society co-chair of the [C2PA Threats and Harms Task Force](https://blog.witness.org/2021/12/witness-and-the-c2pa-harms-and-misuse-assessment-process/), shaping content-provenance standards against surveillance and weaponization risks.

Founded and led the [Prepare, Don't Panic initiative](https://www.gen-ai.witness.org/deepfakes-rapid-response-force/) (2018–) and the Deepfakes Rapid Response Force connecting journalists and fact-checkers with media forensics experts globally. Lead editor of *Video for Change: A Guide for Advocacy and Activism*; [Kennedy Memorial Scholar at Harvard Kennedy School](https://www.witness.org/portfolio_page/sam-gregory/); [2024 PhD from the University of Westminster](https://www.witness.org/portfolio_page/sam-gregory/) on participatory media, AI, and trust. [Testified before the US House and Senate](http://www.samgregory.info/) on AI and synthetic media transparency. Appointed Board Member of the Scott Trust (2026); 2025 Gruber Distinguished Lecturer at Yale Law School; 2026 CAIDP AI Policy Leader Award.
