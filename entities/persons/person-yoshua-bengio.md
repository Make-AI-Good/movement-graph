# Yoshua Bengio

Born in Paris to a Moroccan-Jewish family; Canadian citizen and Full Professor of Computer Sciences at the Université de Montréal since 1993. Founder and Scientific Director of Mila – Quebec AI Institute, the largest university-based deep learning research group in the world. Shared the 2018 ACM A.M. Turing Award with Geoffrey Hinton and Yann LeCun for "conceptual and engineering breakthroughs that have made deep neural networks a critical component of computing."

Key AI safety advocacy actions: co-signed the Future of Life Institute's open letter calling for a six-month pause on training AI systems more powerful than GPT-4 (March 2023); co-signed the [Center for AI Safety's extinction-risk statement](https://www.safe.ai/work/statement-on-ai-risk) (May 2023); testified before the U.S. Senate Judiciary Subcommittee on Privacy, Technology, and the Law on AI threats to democracy and national security (July 2023); attended the UK AI Safety Summit at Bletchley Park (November 2023) and was appointed to chair the International Scientific Report on the Safety of Advanced AI; delivered an interim report at the AI Seoul Summit (May 2024); publicly endorsed ["A Right to Warn about Advanced Artificial Intelligence"](../events/event-openai-employees-safety-letter-2024-06-04.md) alongside [Geoffrey Hinton](../persons/person-geoffrey-hinton.md) and Stuart Russell (June 2024); founded LawZero, a non-profit AI safety research organization (June 2025); and was appointed co-chair of the UN Independent International Scientific Panel on AI (February 2026).
