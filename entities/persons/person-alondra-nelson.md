# Alondra Nelson

American sociologist and science policy scholar; Harold F. Linder Professor at the Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton and director of its Science, Technology, and Social Values Lab; former Principal Deputy Director for Science and Society at the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP, 2021–2023), serving as Acting Director from February to October 2022 — the [first African American and first woman of color to lead US science and technology policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alondra_Nelson). She is the architect of the [Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/ostp/ai-bill-of-rights/) (October 2022), the first US federal framework articulating rights-based principles for AI systems. Before her government role she served as president and CEO of the Social Science Research Council (2017–2021) and previously as first Dean of Social Science at Columbia University. Board member at [Data & Society](../organizations/org-data-and-society.md) 2016–2021; Distinguished Senior Fellow at the Center for American Progress (March 2023–); appointed to the UN High-Level Advisory Body on Artificial Intelligence (October 2023).
