# Ed Newton-Rex

British composer and music-technology entrepreneur; Founder and CEO of [Fairly Trained](../organizations/org-fairly-trained.md), the international nonprofit certifying generative AI models that train only on licensed data. Newton-Rex [founded Jukedeck](https://completemusicupdate.com/stability-ais-vp-of-audio-resigns-over-its-position-that-training-ai-with-copyright-works-is-fair-use/), one of the first music-making AI companies, and sold it to TikTok/ByteDance in 2019, subsequently serving as Product Director of TikTok's in-house AI Lab and then Chief Product Officer at music app Voisey (acquired by Snap in 2020). He joined Stability AI in 2022 as VP of Audio, where his team developed Stable Audio using licensed training data, and [resigned in November 2023](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/why-just-resigned-from-my-job-generative-ai/) after publicly disagreeing with the company's and the broader AI industry's position that training on copyrighted material without consent is permissible under fair use.
