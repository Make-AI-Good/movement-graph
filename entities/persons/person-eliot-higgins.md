# Eliot Higgins

Founder and executive director of [Bellingcat](../organizations/org-bellingcat.md), the Amsterdam-based open-source intelligence investigative organization he [founded in July 2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eliot_Higgins) from self-taught analysis of weapons footage from the Syrian Civil War published on his personal blog "Brown Moses." Previously worked in office administration without formal journalism training; Higgins demonstrated that publicly available imagery, satellite data, and social media archives could produce investigative conclusions ahead of traditional institutional reporting.

Led or directed Bellingcat's landmark OSINT investigations — identifying the Russian Buk unit responsible for downing Malaysia Airlines Flight 17 (2014), naming the GRU officers behind the Salisbury novichok poisoning (2018), and tracing the FSB surveillance team that followed Alexei Navalny — the methodology later applied to Bellingcat's deepfake-abuse investigation strand exposing non-consensual deepfake platforms. Named one of [TIME's 100 Most Influential People in 2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eliot_Higgins). Author of *We Are Bellingcat: An Intelligence Agency for the People* (Bloomsbury, 2021).
