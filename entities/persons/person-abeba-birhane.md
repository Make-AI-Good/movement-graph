# Abeba Birhane

Abeba Birhane is an Ethiopian-born cognitive scientist and Assistant Professor of Artificial Intelligence at Trinity College Dublin's School of Computer Science and Statistics, where she is founder and Principal Investigator of the [AI Accountability Lab](https://aial.ie/). She holds a Senior Advisor in AI Accountability role at [Mozilla Foundation](../organizations/org-mozilla-foundation.md), following a 15-month Mozilla Senior Fellowship in Trustworthy AI (concluded May 2023). Her research works at the intersection of complex adaptive systems, machine learning, algorithmic bias, and critical race studies, with a particular focus on systematic auditing of AI models and training datasets. She is a member of Ireland's Artificial Intelligence Advisory Council, the UN Secretary-General's High-level Advisory Body on Artificial Intelligence, and the OECD AI Expert Group on Risk and Accountability. She was named to TIME's inaugural TIME100 Most Influential People in AI list in 2023.
