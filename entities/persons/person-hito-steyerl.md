# Hito Steyerl

German filmmaker, moving-image artist, writer, and critical theorist; [born 1 January 1966 in Munich, West Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hito_Steyerl). [PhD in Philosophy from the Academy of Fine Arts Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hito_Steyerl), following study at the Japan Institute of the Moving Image and the University of Television and Film Munich.

Professor of Current Digital Media (Emergent Digital Media) at the Akademie der Bildenden Künste München since 2024; previously Professor of New Media Art at the [Universität der Künste Berlin (UdK)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hito_Steyerl) from 2014 to 2024. [Co-founded the Research Centre for Proxy Politics (2014–2017) at UdK](https://www.estherschipper.com/artists/102-the-work-of-hito-steyerl/biography/) with Vera Tollmann and Boaz Levin, concluding with the publication *Proxy Politics: Power and Subversion in a Networked Age* (Archive Books, Berlin, 2017).

Named [#1 most influential figure in contemporary art by *ArtReview* (2017) and #2 in 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hito_Steyerl). Awards include the [Erich Fromm Prize (2025)](https://www.estherschipper.com/artists/102-the-work-of-hito-steyerl/biography/), [Käthe Kollwitz Prize, Akademie der Künste Berlin (2019)](https://www.estherschipper.com/artists/102-the-work-of-hito-steyerl/biography/), and [EYE Prize (2015)](https://www.estherschipper.com/artists/102-the-work-of-hito-steyerl/biography/). She [declined the Federal Cross of Merit from the German President in 2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hito_Steyerl).
