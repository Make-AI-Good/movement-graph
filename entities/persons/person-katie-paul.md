# Katie Paul

Anthropologist and investigative researcher; Director of the [Tech Transparency Project](../organizations/org-tech-transparency-project.md) since 2020; co-director and co-founder of the Antiquities Trafficking and Heritage Anthropology Research (ATHAR) Project (housed at the Achilles Research Group nonprofit); founding member of the Alliance to Counter Crime Online (ACCO). B.A. from Miami University (Ohio) in Anthropology and Ancient Greek; M.A. in Anthropology from The George Washington University. Voice entry: [voice-katie-paul](../voices/voice-katie-paul.md).
