# TikTok and YouTube Both Approved 100 of Our AI-Generated Election Disinformation Ads

*TikTok and YouTube Both Approved 100 of Our AI-Generated Election Disinformation Ads* is a September 2023 investigation published by [Global Witness](../organizations/org-global-witness.md) under its Digital Threats programme, documenting a complete failure of AI ad review systems on both TikTok and YouTube: every one of 100 AI-generated election disinformation ads submitted by Global Witness was [approved by both platforms](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2023/sep/27/tiktok-youtube-approved-100-ai-election-disinformation-ads-global-witness), in violation of each platform's stated election integrity policies. Published ahead of what Global Witness identified as a 2024 election cycle spanning more than 50 countries, the investigation extended the adversarial testing methodology that Global Witness had developed across earlier platform-accountability investigations — including its 2021 Facebook Tigray conflict disinformation test — to AI-generated content specifically.

## Methodology and findings

[Global Witness submitted 100 AI-generated election disinformation ads](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2023/sep/27/tiktok-youtube-approved-100-ai-election-disinformation-ads-global-witness) to TikTok and YouTube through each platform's standard advertiser interface. The ads contained false claims about voting procedures, election officials, and polling locations — content explicitly prohibited by both platforms' election integrity and advertising policies. [Both TikTok and YouTube approved all submitted content](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2023/sep/27/tiktok-youtube-approved-100-ai-election-disinformation-ads-global-witness) — a zero-detection rate across 100 submissions and both platforms. Global Witness's adversarial testing methodology creates a specific evidentiary record: approval receipts alongside documentation of each ad's explicit policy violations. This establishes not merely that harmful content can circulate on a platform, but that the platform's own formal AI review system actively approved it when submitted through normal advertiser channels. Coverage by [the Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2023/sep/27/tiktok-youtube-approved-100-ai-election-disinformation-ads-global-witness) and BBC made the failure documentation widely visible in pre-election-cycle policy debates.

## Position within the corpus

This investigation is the anchor work of [Global Witness](../organizations/org-global-witness.md)'s AI-generated election disinformation reporting — the investigation that specifically tests AI generation as a production mode for disinformation at platform scale. It belongs to the broader series of Global Witness Digital Threats investigations, which collectively constitute the primary adversarial-testing platform-accountability tradition in the corpus, alongside the 2021 Facebook Tigray test and subsequent investigations in the series. The 2023 investigation is significant as the first in the series to specifically test AI-generated disinformation content: as generative AI tools became widely accessible ahead of the 2024 election cycle, the relevant platform-accountability question shifted from whether disinformation could evade platform review to whether AI-generated disinformation specifically presented any additional challenge — and Global Witness's 100-percent approval rate answered it for TikTok and YouTube.
