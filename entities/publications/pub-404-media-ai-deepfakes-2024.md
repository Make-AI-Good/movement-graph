# AI-Generated Taylor Swift Porn Went Viral on Twitter. Here's How It Got There

*AI-Generated Taylor Swift Porn Went Viral on Twitter. Here's How It Got There* is an [investigative report](https://www.404media.co/ai-generated-taylor-swift-porn-twitter/) published by [404 Media](../organizations/org-404-media.md) on January 25, 2024, written by Emanuel Maiberg and Samantha Cole. The investigation traced the origin and distribution infrastructure of AI-generated non-consensual intimate images of Taylor Swift that had gone viral on Twitter/X — the first major test case for AI-generated NCII at viral scale — and identified Microsoft Designer as the AI tool used by the Telegram community producing the images, placing direct accountability pressure on a major technology company's consumer product.

## The investigation

The images circulated on January 24–25, 2024. One viral example received [45 million views and 24,000 reposts](https://www.404media.co/ai-generated-taylor-swift-porn-twitter/) before the original account was removed after approximately 17 hours. Maiberg and Cole traced the images to two origin points: a 4chan board and a Telegram group explicitly dedicated to generating non-consensual AI intimate images of women. The investigation identified Microsoft Designer — a free Microsoft text-to-image generator available to the public — as among the AI tools the Telegram community used to produce the images, establishing a direct line from a major technology company's consumer product to the synthetic intimate imagery supply chain.

Twitter/X deleted the original poster's account and other accounts sharing similar images following the investigation's publication. Taylor Swift's fan base mounted a coordinated counter-operation, [flooding search results](https://www.404media.co/ai-generated-taylor-swift-porn-twitter/) for the viral phrase with protective content — an organic platform-accountability tactic that reduced image visibility in the platform's own search index. Neither Twitter/X nor Microsoft had moved to address the underlying infrastructure before the investigation surfaced it.

## Platform and legislative outcomes

The investigation produced direct legislative outcomes. Members of Congress sent a letter to Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella citing [404 Media](../organizations/org-404-media.md)'s investigation, pressing the company on its responsibility for Microsoft Designer's role in the NCII supply chain. The Taylor Swift case accelerated congressional attention to AI-generated intimate imagery that co-author Samantha Cole had been documenting since her foundational deepfake reporting at Motherboard in December 2017.

The investigation was the lead piece in a broader 404 Media NCII accountability series that ran through 2024. In April 2024, [404 Media](../organizations/org-404-media.md) published separate investigations into Instagram's advertising of nonconsensual AI nude applications and into AI undress apps available on the Apple App Store; Apple [removed three NCII-enabling applications](https://www.404media.co/apple-removes-nonconsensual-ai-nude-apps-following-404-media-investigation/) after the outlet provided direct links to the apps and their associated advertisements — apps Apple's own review process had failed to detect. A December 2024 bipartisan congressional letter to Apple CEO Tim Cook cited the 404 Media investigation, noting that Apple "was not able to identify the apps on their own" despite the App Store's screening commitments.

## Position within the corpus

This investigation is the corpus's primary documentation of AI-generated NCII as a movement-generating harm at viral scale — the synthetic-media harm episode that most directly mobilized congressional action and platform-accountability pressure in 2024. The Taylor Swift incident's combination of scale (45 million views), celebrity subject, and named corporate tool (Microsoft Designer) made it the most legible single episode for legislators and public audiences who had not previously engaged with AI harm reporting.
