# Humanity AI

Humanity AI is a collaborative philanthropic initiative [announced October 14, 2025](https://omidyar.com/update/humanity-ai/), committing [$500 million over five years](https://www.macfound.org/press/press-releases/humanity-ai-commits-500-million-to-build-a-people-centered-future-for-ai) to ensuring artificial intelligence serves the public good. Co-chaired by the [Omidyar Network](fund-omidyar-network.md) and the [John D. and Catherine T. MacArthur Foundation](fund-macarthur-foundation.md), the founding coalition spans ten foundations: the Doris Duke Foundation, [Ford Foundation](fund-ford-foundation.md), [Kapor Foundation](fund-kapor-foundation.md), Lumina Foundation, Mellon Foundation, [Mozilla Foundation](fund-mozilla-foundation.md), David and Lucile Packard Foundation, and Siegel Family Endowment complete the partnership. [Rockefeller Philanthropy Advisors](https://www.rockpa.org/) serves as fiscal sponsor, managing the pooled grant fund and coordinating funder engagement through a dedicated Executive Director.

The initiative's animating claim, [articulated by Omidyar Network's Michele L. Jawando](https://omidyar.com/update/humanity-ai/), is that ["AI is not destiny, it is design"](https://omidyar.com/update/humanity-ai/) — that the central question is not whether AI will transform society but whether people and communities outside Silicon Valley will have meaningful participation in determining how. The [Humanity AI website](https://humanityai.ai/) frames the goal as building "a more human(e) future in which AI is shaped by and for people."

## Five domains, three levers

Humanity AI's grantmaking and foundation-portfolio work run across [five programmatic domains](https://humanityai.ai/): Democracy (transparency, accountability, and civic participation in AI governance); Education (directing AI's benefits toward all students equitably); Humanities and Culture (protecting artists' intellectual-property rights while enhancing human creativity); Labor and Economy (workforce preparation and AI that enhances rather than replaces work); and Security (identifying and mitigating AI risks across critical sectors).

Three strategic levers drive the theory of change across those domains: [expanding the diversity of decision-makers in AI design, development, deployment, and governance; strengthening organisations that amplify community voices in AI's future; and supporting public goods that democratise AI access and control](https://humanityai.ai/). The operative funding standard is ["community-led efforts"](https://humanityai.ai/press-release-open-call-for-grants/) — work in which communities define goals and drive design.

## Grantmaking: inaugural round and open call

[Partner foundations began aligned grantmaking across their respective portfolios in fall 2025](https://omidyar.com/update/humanity-ai/). The pooled fund's inaugural grant round, [announced in May 2026](https://humanityai.ai/humanity-ai-announces-more-than-18-million-in-new-grants-to-shape-ai-for-the-public-good/), distributed more than $18 million in two streams.

A first stream of [$8 million](https://humanityai.ai/humanity-ai-announces-more-than-18-million-in-new-grants-to-shape-ai-for-the-public-good/) went to nine organisations at $500,000 each: the [AI Now Institute](../organizations/org-ai-now-institute.md), Center for Democracy and Technology, Council on Foreign Relations LEAD AI, the [DAIR Institute](../organizations/org-dair-institute.md), Partnership on AI, TechEquity, Kinfolk Tech, Pulitzer Center, and Student Defense. The announced focus areas for this stream — safeguarding democratic institutions, protecting workers' rights, strengthening journalism, and advancing education — map the initiative's five programmatic domains onto existing civil-society infrastructure. A [$3 million grant](https://humanityai.ai/humanity-ai-announces-more-than-18-million-in-new-grants-to-shape-ai-for-the-public-good/) established AI Civics, led by [Data & Society](../organizations/org-data-and-society.md) with the Digital Public Library of America as anchor partner, to advance public education, literacy, and community decision-making on AI.

A second [$10 million open call](https://humanityai.ai/press-release-open-call-for-grants/) targets US-based nonprofits (deadline October 21, 2026) across Education, Labor and Economy, and Humanities/Arts/Culture. The call [explicitly invites organisations "who have not typically seen themselves as having a role in shaping AI"](https://humanityai.ai/press-release-open-call-for-grants/) — workers, artists, students, educators — and [prioritises proposals using open-weight and open-source models](https://humanityai.ai/press-release-open-call-for-grants/) where AI is central to the work, a structural preference that distinguishes this call from other AI philanthropy.
